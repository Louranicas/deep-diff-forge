# Code of Conduct

Deep-Diff-Forge adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
the most widely used code of conduct in open source. The full text at that link is
binding. This page summarises it and says how to reach us.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone. That
holds regardless of:

- age, body size, or visible or invisible disability;
- ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression;
- level of experience, education, or socio-economic status;
- nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
  orientation.

We act and interact in ways that keep this an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

## What we don't accept

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space:

- issues, pull requests and discussions;
- review comments and commit messages;
- anywhere someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Louranicas). For anything that is also a
security matter, use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Louranicas/deep-diff-forge/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer follows the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines),
which escalate from a private correction to a permanent ban.
