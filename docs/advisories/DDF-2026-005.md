# DDF-2026-005: a CI privilege grant written with quotes was not found

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Severity | Not rated by an assessment. A released ranking signal missed what it states it finds: a workflow that gains a write permission or inherited secrets, written in a spelling YAML reads the same as one the signal lists. The patch's paths, sections, hunks and lines were shown as before; the label `ci_privilege` and its +4 were not |
| Affected | 0.8.2 to 0.9.0. Measured on 2026-11-22 on the published 0.8.2 and 0.8.3 binaries, the 0.8.4 release binary (`0c93b3ed…`) and the 0.9.0 release binary (`83158afe…`); earlier releases were not measured |
| Fixed in | 0.10.0. What stays open is under "What 0.10.0 does not change" |
| Found | 2026-11-18, by the coordinator while checking the deep hardening investigation of 0.9.0 (`FINDINGS.md` section 0.2), which had listed it among the equal spellings missed |
| Published | With the tag `v0.10.0` |

## What went wrong

`ci_privilege` reads an added line of a workflow (`.github/workflows/*.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`)
for listed tokens: `pull_request_target`, `write-all`, `contents: write`, `id-token: write`,
`packages: write`, `actions: write`, `secrets: inherit`. The reader keeps a YAML quoted scalar
with its quotes, so a token that is a key and a value matched only its plain spelling. To YAML,
`contents: 'write'`, `contents: "write"`, `"contents": "write"`, `{contents: 'write'}` and
`contents : write` are the same mapping as `contents: write`. A formatter, a generator that
writes JSON (which YAML reads) or an agent may write any of them. The single-word tokens
(`pull_request_target`, `write-all`) were found in quotes, since the quote is no part of a word.

## What this could cause

A workflow change that grants a write permission or passes on the caller's secrets ranks
without the label and its +4, so a reviewer who relies on the ranking or the label may look
elsewhere first. Measured on 2026-11-22 (`--stdin-patch --rank --json`), each form for each of
the five key-value tokens; "the fix" is the 0.10.0 candidate (commit `d20dc680`):

| Spelling in a workflow | 0.8.2 | 0.8.3 | 0.8.4 | 0.9.0 | the fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `contents: write` (control) | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `permissions: {contents: write}` (control) | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `contents: 'write'`, `contents: "write"` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `'contents': write`, `"contents": write` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `"contents": "write"`, `'contents': 'write'` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `{contents: 'write'}`, `{"contents": "write"}` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `contents : write`, `contents:  write` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| `contents: 'write'  # why` | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |

`network_fetch` on YAML (`curl`, `wget`), `secret_like` and `build_hook` on `package.json` were
measured in the same forms and found every spelling YAML or JSON allows, in every version, but
the limits below.

## How to check a version

`deep-diff-forge --version` prints the version. Rank this patch (`deep-diff-forge
--stdin-patch --rank --json`); nothing is applied, so it is safe to run:

```text
diff --git a/.github/workflows/release.yml b/.github/workflows/release.yml
--- a/.github/workflows/release.yml
+++ b/.github/workflows/release.yml
@@ -1,2 +1,4 @@
 on: push
+permissions:
+  contents: 'write'
 jobs:
```

0.8.2 to 0.9.0 give the file score 2 and the signals `config_or_lockfile` only; 0.10.0 adds
`ci_privilege` at new line 3 (score 6).

## What 0.10.0 changes

- A YAML text is read a second time with each quoted scalar as its content and the blanks
  around a key's `:` as one space after it. The bytes of the line say what is a scalar: a quote
  at the start of the line or after a blank, `[`, `{`, `,` or a quoted key's `:`, closed on the
  same line before a byte that can end a scalar, with no escape inside. Nothing is parsed, and
  comments stay blanked, so `# contents: 'write'` is still not a grant.
- What the two readings find is united. The first reading is the one 0.9.0 had, so no line has
  fewer items than before. Measured over 3,580 patches of this repository's and forge-warden's
  history and the 202 patches that remain of the 6,110-patch corpus: no item lost and none
  gained; over 767 crafted patches (the hardening investigation's cases, the forms above and
  the evaluation corpus): 61 items gained, each a quoted or spaced grant, none lost.

## What 0.10.0 does not change

- **Spellings that are not read**, stated in the `ci_privilege` and `build_hook` rows of
  [docs/reference/risk-ranking.md](../reference/risk-ranking.md): a value with an escape
  (`"\x77rite"`), a tag (`!!str write`), a block scalar (`contents: >-` and the value on the
  next line), an explicit key (`? contents` and `: write` on the next line). In `package.json`
  a `\u` escape in a key (`"install"`) is not read by `build_hook`.
- A grant assembled elsewhere (a reusable workflow's defaults, an organisation's settings) is
  not in the patch.
- No evidence means no token was found, never that a change is safe.

## What to do

1. Use 0.10.0 from the signed tag `v0.10.0`: its release archive checked against the signed
   `SHA256SUMS` (`scripts/release_local.sh verify`), or a build of the tag.
2. Until then, and for patches already reviewed with 0.8.2 to 0.9.0, search the workflow files
   for these keys with the value `write` or `inherit` in any quoting or spacing, for example
   `grep -nE "(contents|id-token|packages|actions|secrets)['\"]? *: *['\"]?(write|inherit)" .github/workflows/*.yml .gitlab-ci.yml`,
   and read each hit as a grant (the plain spelling, which every version found, matches too).

## Found by

The deep hardening investigation of 0.9.0 (2026-11-18) listed 130 equal spellings that no rule
reads, nearly all outside the spellings the README listed (now
[docs/reference/risk-ranking.md](../reference/risk-ranking.md)). The coordinator checked it and found
that this one is not: the README listed `contents: write`, and `contents: 'write'` is the same
YAML. Measured again on the published and release binaries before this advisory was written.
